Three things we believe

  1. Attention is a material. The small decisions are usually the ones that make a thing feel alive.
  2. Useful can still be strange. There is no reason an everyday object cannot have a private sense of humor.
  3. Good work leaves room. We make pieces that invite your meaning in instead of explaining everything away.
